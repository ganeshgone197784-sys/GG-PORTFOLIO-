A developer who cares about the whole stack

I'm Ganesh Gone, a full stack developer who enjoys the entire arc of building software — sketching the data model, wiring up the API, and polishing the interface until it feels inevitable.

Most of my recent work sits at the intersection of React/Next.js front ends and Node.js back ends, deployed on modern serverless infrastructure. I like small, well-tested codebases more than clever ones.

Outside of client and product work, I contribute to a couple of open-source tools and write about performance and developer experience.
